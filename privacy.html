NOTA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NOTA ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use the NOTA mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Who We Are

NOTA is operated by Hlib Ladin, an individual developer based in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. You can contact us at bbs7755@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Account information: email address, display name
  • Tasks, notes, habits, and journal entries you create
  • Financial data: budget categories, manual transactions you enter
  • Mood and productivity scores you log
  • Bank account connections (via Plaid — see Section 5)

Information collected automatically:

  • App usage data (features accessed, session duration)
  • Device information (operating system, device type)
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Subscription and purchase status (via RevenueCat)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App and its features
  • To sync your data across devices via Supabase cloud storage
  • To power AI features (task suggestions, focus debrief, AI chat)
  • To process in-app subscriptions and restore purchases
  • To automatically import and categorize bank transactions (if you connect your bank)
  • To send you habit reminders and notifications you have enabled
  • To improve the App and fix bugs

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers using row-level security (RLS), meaning only your account can access your data. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Bank Connection (Plaid)

If you choose to connect your bank account, we use Plaid Technologies, Inc. to securely link your account. Plaid acts as an intermediary — your bank credentials are entered directly on Plaid's interface and never stored on our servers. We only receive transaction data and account balances from Plaid, which is displayed in your personal budget dashboard and is never shared with third parties.

Plaid's privacy policy is available at plaid.com/legal/privacy-notice.

6. AI Features (Anthropic)

AI-powered features (AI Chat, Focus Debrief, task suggestions) are powered by Anthropic's Claude API. When you use these features, relevant context (such as your task list or focus session notes) is sent to Anthropic's servers to generate a response. This data is used solely to generate the response and is subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy. We do not use your data to train AI models.

7. Subscriptions (RevenueCat)

In-app subscription management is handled by RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat processes purchase receipts to verify and manage your subscription status. No financial payment data (credit card numbers, etc.) is processed by us — all payments go through the Apple App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the App's features. You can delete all your data at any time from Settings → Data & Export → Delete All Data within the App. Account deletion removes all personal data from our servers within 30 days.

Bank data: If you disconnect a linked bank account, we stop receiving data for that account and delete the associated Plaid access token and imported financial data. We only retain financial data for as long as the connection is active or as required by law.

This data retention and deletion policy is enforced through the controls described above and is reviewed at least annually to ensure it remains compliant with applicable data privacy laws (PIPEDA, GDPR, CCPA). For more detail on our security practices, see our Information Security Policy.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data (via in-app settings or by contacting us)
  • Export your data (via Settings → Data & Export)
  • Withdraw consent at any time (by deleting your account)

Canadian users are protected under PIPEDA. EU/EEA users are protected under the GDPR. California users are protected under the CCPA. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at bbs7755@gmail.com.

11. Children's Privacy

NOTA is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
