NOTA — Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026 · Owner: Hlib Ladin · Calgary, Alberta, Canada · bbs7755@gmail.com

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines how NOTA ("we", "us", or "our") retains and deletes consumer personal data, including financial data accessed through Plaid. It applies to all consumer data collected and processed by the NOTA mobile application and its backend. The policy is designed to comply with applicable data privacy laws, including PIPEDA (Canada), GDPR (EU/EEA), and CCPA (California).

2. Principles

  • Data minimization: We collect and retain only the data necessary to provide the application's features.
  • Purpose limitation: Data is retained only for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law.
  • User control: Users can review, export, and delete their data at any time.

3. Retention Periods

Data categoryRetention
Account data (email, display name)Retained while the account is active; deleted on account deletion.
User-generated content (tasks, notes, habits, budgets)Retained while the account is active; deleted on account deletion.
Financial data imported via Plaid (transactions, balances)Retained only while the bank connection is active. Deleted when the user disconnects the bank or deletes the account.
Plaid access tokensDeleted immediately when the user disconnects the bank or deletes the account.
Crash logs / diagnostic dataRetained for a limited period for debugging, then automatically expired.

4. Deletion Mechanisms

  • In-app deletion: Users can delete all their data at any time from Settings → Data & Export → Delete All Data.
  • Bank disconnection: Disconnecting a linked bank removes the associated Plaid access token and imported financial data.
  • Account deletion: Deleting an account removes all associated personal data from our production systems within 30 days.
  • Requests by email: Users may also request access, correction, export, or deletion of their data by contacting bbs7755@gmail.com.

5. Storage and Security

Consumer data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (Supabase) protected by row-level security, encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and encrypted at rest using AES-256. Bank login credentials are entered directly within Plaid Link and are never stored on our systems. Further detail is available in our Information Security Policy (https://lilg1488.github.io/nota-legal/security.html).

6. Review

This policy is reviewed and updated at least annually, or sooner when material changes occur to the application, infrastructure, or applicable laws.
